# Outreach toolkit

A reusable playbook for reaching the right people. Personalise the first line every time, always lead with a number, always link the portfolio.

Links to paste:

- Hire one-pager: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/hire
- GitHub: https://github.com/AChris-T
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/akinfenwa-taiwo-christopher/

## Who to target (priority order)

1. **Hiring managers / engineering leads** at companies with open remote full-stack or React/Node roles. The person you'd report to, not only the recruiter.
2. **Founders / CTOs at early-stage startups.** They hire fast and value "built it solo, end-to-end", which is your strongest story.
3. **Recruiters who posted a role you fit.** Comment on the post, then DM.
4. **People who recently posted about hiring** on LinkedIn (search "hiring full-stack remote").

## Cadence

- 5 personalised messages a day beats 50 generic ones.
- Follow up once after 4-5 days if no reply. Then stop.
- Track who you contacted and when (a simple spreadsheet is enough).

## Templates

### LinkedIn connection note (max 300 characters)
> Hi [Name], I'm a full-stack engineer (React, TypeScript, NestJS, Postgres). I recently shipped a production platform solo: a 583-endpoint API, live and in use. Keen to connect and follow [Company]'s work.

### Cold message to a hiring manager / founder
> Hi [Name], I saw [Company] is building [what they do]. I'm a full-stack engineer who ships end-to-end, most recently a Next.js + NestJS platform (583 endpoints, 129 models) built on my own, plus AI features in production at Viro. 20-second overview: [hire link]. Would a quick chat be worth your time?

### Follow-up after applying to a role
> Hi [Name], I applied for [role] and wanted to reach out directly. The [specific requirement from the post] maps closely to [your specific project]. Quick overview: [hire link]. Happy to answer anything.

### Reply when a role can't sponsor / is location-locked
> Thanks for letting me know, I appreciate the quick reply. If you come across roles open to candidates outside [country], as a contractor or via an employer-of-record, I'd love to be considered. I already work remotely for a UK company. Happy to stay in touch.

### Cover note for a job application email
> Hello, I'd like to apply for [role]. I'm a full-stack engineer with 5+ years in React, Node.js and TypeScript, and I do my best work owning features from architecture to production. [One line matching their top requirement to your experience.] CV attached, more here: [hire link]. Thank you for your time.
